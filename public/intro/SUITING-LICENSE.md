# Photographic suiting material and studio

Fabric083 — ambientCG / Lennart Demes, CC0 1.0 Universal.
Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric083
License: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/
Download: https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Fabric083_4K-PNG.zip

Provider creation method: PBRMultiAngle (height field photogrammetry).
Native maps are 4096 × 4096; OpenGL normal and displacement PNGs are 16-bit.
The fiber composition and physical swatch dimensions are unspecified; do not
claim certified pure wool or measured dimensions. Runtime uses 2048² / 1024².

The source has gray/white check yarns. Broad pigment differences were replaced
with uniform black dye; photographed fine luminance variation is retained.
Normal and roughness were downsampled, renormalized and packed without inventing
weave, painting highlights or generating noise. Artistic UV swatch scale: 0.12m.
Hashes and preparation details: suiting-scan-manifest.json, prepare-suiting-scan.py.

Studio Small 09 — Sergej Majboroda / Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09 · https://polyhaven.com/license
Real Radiance HDR was downsampled in linear half-float and prefiltered offline.
The radiance range is retained (maximum 440.5); no clamp to an 8-bit PNG and no
runtime PMREM work. Details: studio-hdr-license.json, bake-hdr-studio.py.

The previous Poly Haven wool scan is retained as a superseded asset and is no
longer requested by the scene. Its separate original license remains unchanged.
